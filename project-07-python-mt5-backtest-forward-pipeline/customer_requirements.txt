PORTFOLIO PROJECT - SIMULATED CLIENT REQUIREMENT

Title: Python MT5 Trading Bot – Backtest to Forward-Test Pipeline

Business need:
The client has a rule-based trading strategy and wants a maintainable Python implementation
that can be researched on historical data, tested in paper/forward mode, and connected to MT5.

Required components:
1. Deterministic strategy module.
2. Risk-based position sizing.
3. One-position-at-a-time execution logic.
4. Historical / paper simulation.
5. Trade logging.
6. Error-safe MT5 integration layer.
7. Telegram-ready notifications.
8. Clear operational checklist before live deployment.

Portfolio boundaries:
- No broker credentials are included.
- Live orders are disabled by default.
- The repository runs in paper mode.
- The included MT5 adapter demonstrates integration structure but must be tested
  against the client's broker symbol, filling mode and volume rules before live use.
