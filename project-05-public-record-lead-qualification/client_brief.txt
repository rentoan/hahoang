PORTFOLIO PROJECT - SIMULATED CLIENT REQUIREMENT

Title: Public Record / Sheriff Deed Lead Qualification Pipeline

Business need:
A client has a CSV of potential foreclosure-surplus leads and needs to qualify them using
the actual deed/document contents rather than relying only on third-party metadata.

Qualification rule:
QUALIFIED if:
1. Final sale price - foreclosure debt >= $35,000
AND
2. Purchaser is a third party rather than the foreclosing lender / creditor.

Required workflow:
Lead CSV
-> locate corresponding deed document
-> extract sale price, debt and purchaser
-> calculate surplus
-> classify purchaser
-> Qualified / Not Qualified
-> export structured lead data

Important:
If extraction or purchaser classification is uncertain, the record must be sent to a manual-review queue.
The system should not guess.

Portfolio scope:
This version uses synthetic text representations of recorded deeds.
OCR, live county-site automation, authentication, CAPTCHA handling and large-scale crawling are intentionally out of scope.
